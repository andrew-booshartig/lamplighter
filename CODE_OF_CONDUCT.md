# Code of conduct

This project studies religion, and the people who read it and write to it hold beliefs, and doubts, that matter deeply to them. So:

- Assume good faith. Ask before you assume what someone meant.
- Argue from sources. A disagreement about a fact is settled by a source both of you can open, not by who is louder.
- Do not mock, belittle or pressure anyone for what they believe or do not believe, and do not use the project to convert anyone to anything or away from anything.
- No harassment, no personal attacks, no slurs, no discrimination, no publishing other people's private information.
- Say what you know and how you know it, and say plainly what you do not know.

Anyone who breaks these may have their comments hidden or removed and may be blocked from the repository. To report a problem, use GitHub's "Report content" on the comment or issue, or open a private security advisory (Security tab, "Report a vulnerability") if it is serious. This is a short statement written for this project; it will be refined as the project grows.
