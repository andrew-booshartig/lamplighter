What this does not cover
------------------------
A licence cannot take a public-domain text out of the public domain, and cannot give
rights to a text it does not own. The 487 texts in texts/ and the Bibles pack in bibles/
each keep their own status (public domain or openly licensed English translations; the
Universal Declaration of Human Rights keeps the United Nations' own terms of use, and the
transcription of Madison's Memorial and Remonstrance comes from a University of Chicago
Press site whose page is marked (c) 1987, though Madison's own words of 1785 are public domain),
recorded with their translator and source in texts/LICENSES.txt and bibles/LICENSES.txt.
Quotations from other works in the notes are credited where they appear.
